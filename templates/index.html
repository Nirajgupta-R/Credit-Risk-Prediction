About the Model

Enter an applicant's financial details to estimate their likelihood of loan default. The calibrated XGBoost model analyzes the submitted information and compares the estimated default probability with the selected decision threshold to classify the applicant as Default Risk or No Default Risk. The prediction is an estimate, not a guarantee of future repayment behavior.
